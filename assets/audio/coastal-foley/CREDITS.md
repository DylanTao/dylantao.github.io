# Coastal home contact recordings

The mono contact sprite contains brief edited recordings. The edits trim each contact, remove air and rumble outside the useful band, add edge fades and normalize peaks. The dry footsteps have alternating takes.

- Stone footsteps by Fantozzi. CC0 1.0. [Fantozzi’s Footsteps](https://opengameart.org/content/fantozzis-footsteps-grasssand-stone)
- Wooden footsteps edited by TinyWorlds from pdsounds recordings. CC0 1.0. [Different steps on wood, stone, leaves, gravel and mud](https://opengameart.org/content/different-steps-on-wood-stone-leaves-gravel-and-mud)
- Sand, grass and wading footsteps by the Blender Foundation from _Yo Frankie!_. [Sand Step Movement Sounds](https://lpc.opengameart.org/content/sand-step-movement-sounds-yo-frankie), [Grass Foot Step Sounds](https://lpc.opengameart.org/content/grass-foot-step-sounds-yo-frankie), [Water Step Splashes](https://lpc.opengameart.org/content/water-step-splashes-yo-frankie), [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)
- Foliage by qubodup. CC0 1.0. [20 Rustles of dry leaves](https://lpc.opengameart.org/content/20-rustles-dry-leaves)
