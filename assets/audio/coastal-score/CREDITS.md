# Coastal house scores

The five chamber pieces and fifteen short interaction phrases are original compositions and arrangements for Sirui's site, performed with acoustic instrumental samples from the Philharmonia Orchestra. Their library permits use in original compositions. The site distributes the finished mixes. [Philharmonia sound samples and terms](https://philharmonia.co.uk/resources/sound-samples/)

The scores, recording choices and private reproduction instructions are kept with the house's design notes. [World music](../../../docs/house/world-music.md)
