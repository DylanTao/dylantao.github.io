# Coastal house scores

The five chamber pieces, fifteen short interaction phrases and the film score _The Door Is Open_ are original compositions and arrangements for Sirui's site. The instrumental performances use acoustic samples from the Philharmonia Orchestra. Their library permits use in original compositions. [Philharmonia sound samples and terms](https://philharmonia.co.uk/resources/sound-samples/)

The waves at dawn, dusk and night in _The Door Is Open_ come from _ocean-LaJolla.wav_ by Xavier Serra (xserra). He recorded from a cliff overlooking the Pacific in La Jolla on January 28, 2015. The film uses excerpts with a low and high frequency roll-off, fades and a level adjustment. [Original stereo field recording](https://freesound.org/people/xserra/sounds/262593/) · [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

The scores, recording choices and private reproduction instructions are kept with the house's design notes. [World music](../../../docs/house/world-music.md)
