# Original coastal neighbours

`bin/build_coastal_neighbors.py` independently authors `neighbors.blend` and the Raccoon, WesternGull and Sandpiper GLBs in Blender 4.5.9 LTS. These are original continuous profiles, shaped faces, articulated legs, webbed/toed feet and cambered feather fans. No downloaded mesh, commercial texture, generated image or scenic backdrop is used. The existing rabbit, sea-lion and seal masters are retained.

The source borrows the site's own mesh/sculpt helpers from `build_coastal_wildlife.py`. Static eye shades and feather-tip shades become vertex colours and merge under their acting pivots. The editable source retains independent canonical Head, EyeL/EyeR, Muzzle, limb and wing pivots in each export. The live inspection API caches those references once and exposes actual named eye/muzzle world points for face visibility checks.

`wildlife-neighbor-motion.mjs` samples authored active scene time. Raccoon translation follows the existing clifftop path; stance paws hold world anchors, swing feet lift and two-joint legs follow their paw targets. Sandpipers keep the existing three beach passes, alternate planted toes and make a gradual pivot near each zero-speed reversal. Gulls use the original ellipses and perches with velocity/acceleration-continuous quintic transfers, derived headings, gradual flare/fold, and a short departure turn. The balcony visitor stays at its original point.

These are deterministic authored contacts and acting. They are not measured animal biomechanics, aerodynamic flight, a general collision solver, or continuous terrain/feather simulation. Raccoon foot heights interpolate the existing authored path's ground samples after removing the source's 25 mm clifftop clearance; lateral terrain is approximated by a fitted local support plane. Torso and hips accommodate planted paws with a small crouch along the support normal (at most 40.3 mm in the four-lap native check); shared limb reach stretch stays at or below 4.5%. Root routes and world foot anchors stay fixed. Shorebird foot heights use the shared beach samples. Perches retain the original exported ground point. Folded feather fans overlap through bounded pose scaling, rather than simulating individual feathers.

Rebuild from the repository root with Blender `--background --python-exit-code 1 --python bin/build_coastal_neighbors.py`. Only the three neighbour GLBs and editable source are written. Runtime counts remain one raccoon, four flying/perching gulls, one balcony gull and three sandpipers. Realistic remains the only active treatment; this pass introduces no public control row.
