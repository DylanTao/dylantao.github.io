# Original coastal neighbours

`bin/build_coastal_neighbors.py` independently authors `neighbors.blend` and the Raccoon, WesternGull and Sandpiper GLBs in Blender 4.5.9 LTS. These are original continuous profiles, shaped faces, articulated legs, webbed/toed feet and cambered feather fans. No downloaded mesh, commercial texture, generated image or scenic backdrop is used. The existing rabbit, sea-lion and seal masters are retained.

The source borrows the site's own mesh/sculpt helpers from `build_coastal_wildlife.py`. Static eye shades and feather-tip shades become vertex colours and merge under their acting pivots. The editable source retains independent canonical Head, EyeL/EyeR, Muzzle, limb and wing pivots in each export. The live inspection API caches those references once and exposes actual named eye/muzzle world points for face visibility checks.

`wildlife-neighbor-motion.mjs` samples authored active scene time. Raccoon translation follows the existing clifftop path; stance paws hold world anchors, swing feet lift and two-joint legs follow their paw targets. Sandpipers keep the existing three beach passes, alternate planted toes and make a gradual pivot near each zero-speed reversal. Gulls use the original ellipses and perches with velocity/acceleration-continuous quintic transfers, derived headings, gradual flare/fold, and a short departure turn. The balcony visitor stays at its original point.

These are deterministic authored contacts and acting. They are not measured animal biomechanics, aerodynamic flight, a general collision solver, or continuous terrain/feather simulation. Raccoon foot heights interpolate the existing authored path's ground samples after removing the source's 25 mm clifftop clearance; lateral terrain is approximated by a fitted local support plane. Torso and hips accommodate planted paws with a small crouch along the support normal (at most 40.3 mm in the four-lap native check); shared limb reach stretch stays at or below 4.5%. Root routes and world foot anchors stay fixed. Shorebird foot heights use the shared beach samples. Folded feather fans overlap through bounded pose scaling, rather than simulating individual feathers.

Gull perch contacts are separately authored by `bin/build_coastal_perch_support.py` from the retained physical coast/gallery rail and decoded `WesternGull.glb`. Its small JSON contains bounded local support triangles, sole samples and native ankle/tarsus dimensions. Runtime fits each webbed foot to that physical surface, keeps tarsus lengths fixed and adjusts hidden hips within the original torso. Coast root heights use the measured surface while retaining exported perch x/z; the balcony root and yaw stay unchanged. The rail supports only the parts of the web above its narrow cylinder; toe overhang is intentional. Quintic contact-pose transitions retain the C2 root approach/departure. Local unilateral foot clearance handles flare/toe-off near the perch; it is not collision avoidance for the entire flight or body.

Support patches use the retained mesh's loop triangles, matching its GLB tessellation. Re-triangulating nonplanar quads with a different BVH diagonal can change a contact height by millimetres. Four patches contain 51 triangles in total; each native foot has 192 projected lower-envelope sole samples. The 23,427-byte formatted JSON carries hashes of both source geometry and gull GLB. Surface coefficients/normals and stationary contact poses are cached; animated flare/toe-off samples only these bounded patches. The decoded-geometry check spans 337 approach, perch and departure poses over four cycles. Worst sampled penetration is 1.91 micrometres, ankle separation 6.47 micrometres and hidden hip accommodation 46.8 mm, with unchanged tarsus lengths. These are sampled geometric checks, not a guarantee between samples or over arbitrary future terrain.

Rebuild from the repository root with Blender `--background --python-exit-code 1 --python bin/build_coastal_neighbors.py`. The three neighbour GLBs, editable source and perch-support JSON are written. To refresh only contact data after retained coastal geometry changes, run `bin/build_coastal_perch_support.py` with the same Blender flags. It never saves the coast or rewrites the shared manifest. Runtime counts remain one raccoon, four flying/perching gulls, one balcony gull and three sandpipers. Realistic remains the only active treatment; this pass introduces no public control row.
