The experimental print prompt requested a panoramic Pacific coastline with ochre sandstone cliffs, distant ocean, agave, orange sky, bold ink contours, crosshatching, halftones, and expressive color separation; isolated alpha edges, no text, no people, and no buildings. The concept boards guided composition and identity, rather than being treated as finished meshes or research figures. Sirui subsequently rejected using an image behind the homepage scene, so the print is used only in the separate depth experiment. Every homepage view uses actual water and cliff geometry.

The cartoon options are original personal interpretations of the named animation styles. No studio model, character mesh, film frame, or commercial texture is bundled. Style names describe the requested visual studies; no affiliation is implied.

The supplied generated concepts are retained as `reference-home-style-board.png` (generation `exec-da5b9dd0-eca4-4b5b-a225-cfd2590785af`) and `reference-character-study.png` (`exec-6162ab91-136a-417e-ac71-eb72779a0163`). These are art-direction references, not captures of the finished website or approval of the resulting models. The later live-review corrections take precedence over their compositions.

## Rebuild

Run `blender --background --python-exit-code 1 --python bin/build_coastal_home.py` from the repository root. The script writes the editable sources, GLBs, and model-review portraits. Each character source opens on the idle NLA track; choose another track to inspect its clip. The four human variants share bone names; Lizard adds Tail and TailTip. Append `-- --house-only`, `-- --characters-only`, `-- --lizard-only`, or `-- --avatar=ghibli` (any of the five avatar IDs) for a focused rebuild.

The September 12 refinement replaces disconnected body masses with continuous remeshed skin, flowing hair surfaces, fitted clothing, and blended arm/leg weights. Mouths are recessed geometry; Ghibli uses a quieter closed smile. The house has a vaulted stone envelope connected to inland terrain, a cliff descending roughly seven meters to the tide, a sloping crescent beach, and receding headlands. Roof vegetation follows the roof's cutaway visibility. The subsequent Realistic pass replaces the old disconnected relief chips with continuous cliff fluting; illustrated erosion strokes remain a deferred lab experiment.

The installed Microsoft Store launcher denied background execution. The implementation used the official portable Blender 4.5.9 LTS archive from `download.blender.org`, verified against the release SHA-256 list. No Store permissions were changed.

The subsequent Realistic pass adds `bin/coastal_craft.py`: original mesh floorboards, joinery, built-in shelves/books, keyboard keys, ceramic cup, thin curved leaves and stems, cabinet fronts, lanterns, a braided rug, irregular onsen stones, and folded linen. Cliff fluting and erosion are modeled into the continuous surface. Physical-scale procedural material detail, sky/environment light, contact occlusion, shoreline wash, live water reflection, onsen ripples, and soft vapor are authored in the browser. No commercial textures or scenic backdrop images are used.

Three.js r164 postprocessing and Draco loader sources, plus Google's Apache-2.0 Draco decoder, are pinned locally with source hashes in `assets/js/vendor/three-r164/README.md`. The static worker entry point is extracted from that exact loader by `bin/prepare_draco_worker.py`; it works with the existing site CSP. The sea's oblique reflection-camera construction follows the official r164 Water implementation.

Asset creation used Blender's Python API in background mode. Mouse/keyboard control of the native Blender application was unavailable in this Codex session. The `.blend` files are editable geometry and rigs, not generated pictures presented as models. Append `-- --export-only` to read the existing sources and re-export every GLB with Draco compression, restoring all character animation tracks for export.

The full home source is excluded from Jekyll via `artwork/`. Only the optimized browser assets ship with the website.

The September 13 habitat refinement adds `bin/coastal_interiors.py`: original curved wall ribbons, rounded alcove returns, an arched print niche, oak battens, rubber tiles, a four-post rack, loaded barbell with collars, padded bench and two-tier dumbbell stand. `bin/coastal_characters.py` rebuilds the four human variants against the supplied character board, with explicit jaw profiles, inset eyes, off-centre hair sweeps and tapered locks, relaxed sleeves, individual fingers and laced canvas shoes. All ten clips use the established bone/contact convention. The Lizard geometry is retained. The same capybara print replaces every avatar-dependent wall portrait; no generated backdrop was introduced.

## September 13: connected section and likeness correction

`bin/coastal_section.py` replaces the single-level pedestal and arch with two inhabited storeys at 0 and 2.6 meters. It carves the cave into a mainland mesh, verifies the opening with a Blender ray cast, fits a 15-riser stair, and joins the cliff and beach along the same shoreline. Floor offsets and stair circulation are read from the scene manifest. Only the local ceiling lifts for cutaways. The browser supplies sky, sea, waves and reflections; the standalone Blender review shows the authored geometry without those browser effects.

`bin/coastal_hair.py` builds side-parted scalp and collar-length, ear-tucked surfaces. `bin/coastal_clothing.py` builds sleeves from torso openings. The revised figures have different head proportions, shaped jaws, smaller natural eyes, connected trousers, and a continuous weighted Lizard tail with rounded toes and fingers. Ghibli's face and glasses follow the real portrait more closely; the other figures remain exaggerated style interpretations. These are authored models, not a claim of photographic likeness or equivalence to the generated concept illustrations.

`portraits/` contains front, profile and full-body Cycles studies in addition to the original review angle. `reviews/` contains direct Blender views of the house. All authoring studies remain excluded from Jekyll. Asset creation used Blender's Python API; native Blender mouse and keyboard control is unavailable in this session.

After a full house rebuild, `-- --section-only` reuses the local room cache to rebuild floors and coast, and `-- --coast-only` reuses the local section cache for terrain iteration. These caches are optional and gitignored; the full build reconstructs them from source. `bin/render_coastal_review.py` renders the saved house for direct inspection. The public renderer remains Realistic, with the three-style comparison deferred to GPT-7.

## September 13: garden, beach ecology and Pip

`bin/coastal_garden.py` authors the hanging study planter, trailing leaves, cords and bracket, plus larger lounge and kitchen plants. These meshes are saved in `coastal-home.blend` and exported with the relevant room assets. The widened beach reads its 14-meter base depth, cove bulge and shoreline variation from `manifest.json`; Blender and browser contact geometry share those values.

The September 13 Pip and animals were original code-authored geometry. The robot’s room model was replaced on September 14 with an original articulated Blender GLB; see [Pip provenance](../pip/PROVENANCE.md). `assets/js/companion/portrait.mjs` still draws the matching analytic robot for the 2D page. `wildlife.mjs` models two brush rabbits, a raccoon, four flying gulls, a balcony gull and three sandpipers in browser geometry. The garden, beach and revised robot use Blender's background Python API; native application mouse control was unavailable.

Ceramic reflections, colored rim light, soft hover shadows, leaf backlighting/wind, wet sand, broken wave crests, spray, dust and steam are procedural browser effects. Motion uses damped springs, authored paths and particle fields. No physical fluid simulation, unrestricted rigid-body solver or trained animal model is claimed. All remarks are locally authored strings; no remote conversational service is called.

## September 14: reference, geometry, and contact review

`direction/architecture.png` and `direction/character-wildlife.png` are coordinated generated studies requested for this pass. The original PNGs retain their metadata. `direction/provenance.json` records every retained concept's SHA-256 and C2PA model metadata: **gpt-image, version 2.0**. The tool exposed no model selector, so these must not be labeled Images 2.5. Generated studies are distinct from actual Blender renders in `reviews/` and actual browser evidence in the documentation.

`bin/coastal_landscape.py` authors the nonperiodic coastal masses, sloping beach, and exact sampled support data. The version-4 manifest exports each stair tread, floor polygons, habitat paths, shoreline elevations, perches, and tidal basins. `bin/build_coastal_wildlife.py` builds original brush rabbit, California sea lion, and harbor seal masters; `wildlife.blend` retains them. Habitat choices follow the [City of San Diego's Children's Pool information](https://www.sandiego.gov/lifeguards/beaches/pool). Wildlife behavior is authored rest/look/groom/walk/hop and flight/perch motion, not a physical simulation or biological movement model.

The review loop retained neutral-clay and material views with the manifest's exterior and cutaway cameras, then captured actual browser room views. Seated checks exposed missing trouser skin weights, wrongly oriented sleeve openings, and typing hand orientation; these were corrected in the authoring scripts. Walking contacts now use exported tread surfaces instead of the old stair equation. Groundcover uses upward-facing triangles. Planting, habitat paths, and perches are raycast against the actual carved mainland mesh with a Blender BVH, rather than using an approximate pre-carving height or a flat fan across slopes. Sleeves blend through eight rings into rounded cuffs perpendicular to the arms.

After a geometry rebuild, run `bin/bake_coastal_light.py`, then `bin/optimize_coastal_exports.py`. The first retains Cycles AO and indirect diffuse lighting on the editable source; the second exports quantized byte vertex colors and Draco geometry without overwriting the high-detail `.blend`. The browser uses procedural, physical-scale material normals, dynamic sunlight and lamps, bounded ambient contact shadows, and reflections. No baked normal-map texture set, fluid solver, or film-production quality is claimed.

Run `bin/render_coastal_review.py` for matched-camera clay/material views and `bin/render_coastal_contacts.py` for five seated poses plus the animal masters. The full authoring command remains reproducible without the ignored local caches. The public visual direction is Realistic; Architectural and Illustrated remain explicitly deferred GPT-7 experiments.

Inter now replaces Newsreader on the website. Its regular/italic WOFF2 files, pinned source hashes, weight-range conversion, and SIL OFL are in `assets/fonts/inter/`. The retained Newsreader font is historical and no longer loaded.

## September 14 finishing pass: solid cutaway and fitted activities

This pass continues the approved reference boards above. It generated no additional concept images and makes no new image-model claim. Blender 4.5.9 LTS and the existing Python workflow authored the delivered geometry, animation and optimized GLBs.

The old centroid-based roof split left open boundaries. `coastal_section.py` now partitions a closed cave with exact Boolean intersection/difference, welds coincident vertices, fills residual coplanar boundary loops, recalculates normals, and rejects remaining nonmanifold edges. Both exported sections retain closed solids. `coastal_camera.py` samples the final terrain BVH for camera clearance; it also exports cutaway tags and exterior/interior envelopes consumed by `camera.mjs`.

`coastal_furnishing.py` is the canonical final kitchen and gym authoring source. It retains the capybara artwork and adds fitted cabinetry, island, detailed coffee equipment, a paired-door refrigerator, and ocean-facing dining. The rack gains neutral pull-up grips and dip attachments; the bench parks clear of these exercises. Coffee and weight props remain individually tagged through material batching, so the runtime moves the same authored objects between their rest surfaces and the character's hand.

All five character sources were rebuilt with fourteen clips, softer sleeve weighting and revised eyelids. `activities.mjs` runs phase-based approaches and rests and solves residual arm contact drift against exported equipment grips. The poses remain authored stylization; contact correction is not a whole-body dynamics solver.

The production assets were re-exported after Cycles vertex-light baking and Draco/byte-color optimization. `reviews/` retains matched-camera neutral-clay/material views plus seated, reaching, pull-up and dip studies for all five variants. The live exterior orbit, cup and weight transfers, room views and responsive checks are recorded in [the finishing evidence](../../docs/evidence/coastal-finish-2026-09-14/README.md).
