# Measured seated patch for the bed transfer

This interface measures the current five delivered rigs; it does not certify a mattress placement or a later character export. `assets/models/home/character-contacts.json` binds each actual GLB hash and its native source. Regenerate it after the final character exports.

The patch consists of trouser/short vertices whose dominant joint is Hips, or a Thigh within the first 45% of its actual bind-pose hip-to-knee segment. Tail, distal thigh and knee vertices are excluded. The native and pinned GLTF/Draco pipelines measure independently, with the avatar scene/root at identity. Each decoded lounge clip's actual duration is **4.0416665077 seconds**, sampled at 13 explicit times. Native NLA frame/FPS scopes are recorded separately; these samples are not a continuous collision guarantee.

The lower-envelope witnesses retain their material, dominant joint, proximal fraction and root-relative XYZ. All witnesses within 2 mm of each sampled minimum are kept before coordinate deduplication. `contactCenterXZ` is the midpoint of their complete XZ extents, avoiding a vertex-order or tessellation-density centroid. Hips-only and left/right proximal-Thigh height ranges remain separate, so a later leg-IK change must be read back against the actual support.

The measured offsets are approximately 0.503335 m for Lizard and 0.536886–0.536915 m for the four human styles. The largest native/decoded minimum-Y difference is 0.024 mm; the largest two-way nearest witness-sample distance is 0.041 mm. Those are vertex-sample comparisons, not a surface-error bound. No 0.515 m nominal offset or soft compression is included.

To place the patch, rotate its XZ offset by the actual seated facing, subtract that from the actual seat point, and set `rootY = seatTopY - seatOffsetY`. Begin with zero compression. Root/controller acceptance still must prove that the resulting patch lies on the actual mattress polygon, plus supported feet, privacy and recovery. Native rig measurements alone do not prove that complete journey.

`fitSeatedRoot` in `assets/js/home-scene/character-contacts.mjs` implements that placement as a pure helper: `fitSeatedRoot({ contact, seat: [x, actualTopY, z], facing, compression: 0 })` returns the root-position array. The caller owns model-byte binding, the actual support polygon and any later explicit bounded compression. The helper fetches nothing and changes no rig.
