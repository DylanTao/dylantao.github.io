# Rabbit contact fit and the remaining cutaway habitat defect

Source `31412345b` fits each rabbit's actual paw sole to the delivered terrain,
then accommodates its small body and connected legs. The authored root route,
timing, hop height and middle of each airborne phase remain unchanged. A separate
roof-visibility defect remains open; this checkpoint does not finish all wildlife
hovering.

## Contact evidence

The baseline public rabbit in a grazing pose had front-paw gaps of 25.0/26.4 mm
and rear-paw gaps of 15.6/21.3 mm. Other sampled authored poses pushed a toe as
far as 29.6 mm into the delivered surface. The new helper measures the four actual
sole envelopes against retained triangles. It uses a 0.6 mm toe contact margin,
fades support smoothly across takeoff and landing, and preserves intentional
grooming lifts. Incomplete footprints and implausible corrections report an
unfitted contact instead of inventing ground or moving the authored route.

A body envelope from 1,234 actual skin vertices prevents compressed hips/chest
from entering the ground. This is a reduced CPU query set, not a geometry or
render-detail reduction. The leg attachment follows the measured hip-to-foot
axis; every next pose restores its authored transforms. The body margin is
1.6 mm and its correction is bounded. No mesh, material or species-wide vertical
offset was changed.

An independent native check evaluates every delivered skin vertex, not only that
envelope: 742 paired poses at 371 times across both rigs, schedules, contact
states and hop boundaries. The worst final toe/skin gap is numerical roundoff
(-5.33e-15 m); no sampled skin point lacks support. Planted toe gaps are within
0.600002 mm. Root position and authored lift are exact for every paired row;
all 62 middle-flight whole-skin world-vertex hashes are exact. Root/skeleton
digests of the other 26 neighbours are exact at all 371 times.

Focused tests use the actual delivered rabbit and explicit crossed grades,
edge/missing support, microsecond takeoff/landing continuity, and compressed
hip/chest poses. They check toe/skin distance, not just helper fields. They do
not prove every possible future terrain or animation state.

the 26 actual frozen served inputs. Controls include their unchanged bytes;

ordinary Python gzip/JSON tools. `MANIFEST.json` hashes every payload except itself.
