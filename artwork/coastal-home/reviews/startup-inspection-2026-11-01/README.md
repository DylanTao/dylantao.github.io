# Startup inspection, November 1, 2026

This is an inspection of the complete published 02ec57bb3 product, not an optimization or a speedup result. Canonical renderer source was unchanged. All three arrivals ran concurrently with other work, with passive WebGL observers and CDP sampling; arrival avatars differed. Their ready times must not be compared causally.

The owned 4200 preview was seeded from all 1,364 frozen production files, with no missing, additional or changed files. All 181 module source hashes were checked against the Git baseline and compiled bytes against its compiler receipt. The parent receipt and independent coverage record are retained. Native Chromium used D3D11 on the RTX 3080 Ti, actual public Noon and 3D clicks, ordinary Date/performance/RAF, real fonts and scene assets. Only analytics endpoints were suppressed. Screenshots are representative embedded states, not a release appearance matrix.

The unmodified first probe reached ready in 14.047 s (Rick and Morty arrival). It submitted 121 programs before ready and six Walk variants just after ready. None of the observed compile/link/status calls exceeded 0.6 ms; it did not reproduce the historical multi-second blocking GL call. Its `ready` timestamp accidentally overwrote the separately collected ready-evidence object in the JSON writer. The post-ready evidence still retains the original loading-stage records; this omission is not reconstructed.

Current Walk preparation already occurs after the composed view and uses owned asynchronous compile polling. Its plain render only primes clipping on an empty scene. Mirror preparation also uses owned polling now; the historical whole-scene compileAsync wording does not describe this source. The `withoutHead(draw)` profile wrapper includes normal beauty frames even when inactive, so its inclusive cost is not a Walk-preparation measurement.

An observer-only readiness ledger found 64 repeated program resolutions, but their task-boundary time totaled only 1.5 ms. Caching these cannot substantiate a useful improvement. A second observer measured shadow submission: each warmup submitted 29 casters, 11 custom, with no exact geometry/custom-material/caster identity duplicates. Both shadow passes together took 21.4 ms; custom calls took 3.3 ms. Material-batch setup/restoration took 29.3 ms over 148 calls, compared with 434.7 ms inside renderer.compile. No product change was made for either hypothesis.

The queue probe used Ghibli and reached ready in 20.682 s; the shadow probe used South Park and reached ready in 12.654 s. These are concurrent diagnostics, not matched performance pairs. Queue request failures retained coast/home-shell ERR_ABORTED and the shadow probe retained home-shell ERR_ABORTED; the probes did not establish their cause. Console/page errors were empty. Exact raw JSON, profiles, observer sources, producers, logs and screenshots remain here, compressed losslessly where applicable. The separate initial-probe log/JSON are authoritative for its own failures and context.

The first-ready targets and physical-phone startup qualification remain open. A future attempt should isolate the driver's critical compile path under a quiet, same-avatar pair and check later mirrors/Walk for missed programs.
