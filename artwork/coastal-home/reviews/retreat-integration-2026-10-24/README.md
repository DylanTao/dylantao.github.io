public/private circulation, lighter pool court and fitted interior changes.

including UTF-16 log BOMs. No credentials or private analytics are included.

## Source custody and limits

`sourceCommit: e0658c636` in these working-tree reports is the recorded parent,
not the final release commit. The index binds the actual final source/export,
controller, weather, cache, sketch and tool bytes separately, including canonical
LF hashes where applicable. Final `.blend` grounding and export receipts remain
in the sibling [native review](../retreat-2026-10-24/README.md).

All graphics runs report Intel Arc D3D11. Phone-sized screenshots and touch
emulation do not measure physical-phone frame rate or thermal behavior. The
library ladder rolls but is nonclimbable; pool walking stops at the dry coping.
Bedroom adjacency remains partial (about 11.6 metres of approach/upper link).
Angular cliff cuts, the retained laundry placement and the pool's narrower
sideward ocean opening are recorded design tradeoffs. Weather is an expiring
NWS snapshot plus a validated one-day seasonal baseline and authored long-range
simulation, not a SOTA or live-weather claim.
