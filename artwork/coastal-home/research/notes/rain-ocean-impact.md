# Rain impacts: method fit and next experiment

November 1, 2026. Source inspection uses house baseline `8ac01d3f9db91a64984d31260788b87e44de12f1`. This is a reviewed research proposal; no rendering improvement is admitted.

[Jeschke and Wojtan's SIGGRAPH 2023 method](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/prl/shallow-water-simulation/files/Dispersive_Waves_in_SWE.pdf) separates shallow bulk flow from small surface waves, then recombines their velocities. Its demonstrated CUDA setup uses a 512-square grid at one-metre spacing. The paper reports 128 diffusion substeps per update and assigns 87% of simulation time to decomposition. Sections 4.1 and 5 document those choices; section 5.2 also identifies missing surface tension/physical viscosity and limitations around fine seabeds and boundaries. These results do not establish browser cost. The method's water-grid scale and missing small-scale effects make a complete port a poor first experiment for this site's rain.

The model reviewed extracted method/appendix text. The coordinator independently checked the central scale, decomposition-cost and omitted-force claims against the primary publication. Direct download failed in the model worktree; no PDF-byte hash or independent figure-pixel inspection is claimed. Its extraction evidence stays outside Git. No author code or redistribution license was adopted.

The current [ocean contract](../../../../assets/js/home-scene/PHYSICS.md) retains analytic waves, filtered slope detail, bathymetry, optics and reflections. The [rain field](../../../../assets/js/home-scene/coastal-rain.mjs) has 1,024 desktop or 256 compact lines, a four-metre-per-second fall and a seven-second active-time cycle. The controller supplies terrain/roof heights rather than an exposed-water ownership callback. Existing ocean and pool sampling paths have distinct geometry, footprints and boundaries. These are source observations, not rendered proof of detached impacts.

First audit 16 seeded trajectories for one full cycle over open sea, shoreline rock and the pool edge. Record drop/cycle identity, native active time, first downward crossing, actual displaced surface position and owning surface. Reject roof, land, wrap and duplicate events. Preserve a control for camera movement so rain cannot acquire false impacts when its bounded box shifts. Match the contact sampler to the rendered ocean footprint and choppy displacement before adding any response.

Only after that audit should an isolated experiment add a bounded slope response. Retain the current wave/foam/reflection detail and separate pool ownership. Test actual contact frames, continuity through pause/slow frames, disposal, buffers and extra passes. A quiet matched native GPU pair and a physical iPhone comparison are separate performance gates. The retained [FFT attempt](../../../../assets/js/home-scene/FFT-OCEAN.md) and [representation review](../../../../docs/graphics-representation-review-2026-10.md) remain controls; no solver, neural cache or backend replacement is accepted here.

The real model trial produced a longer fit note, but its structured handback failed exact-path validation. That attempt remains failed. This coordinator-edited note preserves the independently checked finding; it does not relabel the model response or close contact/quality-cost gates.
