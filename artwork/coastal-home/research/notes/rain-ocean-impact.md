# Rain impacts: method fit and next experiment

November 1, 2026, with a November 2 UTC implementation follow-up. The original source inspection used house baseline `8ac01d3f9db91a64984d31260788b87e44de12f1`. The method review and the subsequent local implementation have separate evidence.

[Jeschke and Wojtan's SIGGRAPH 2023 method](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/prl/shallow-water-simulation/files/Dispersive_Waves_in_SWE.pdf) separates shallow bulk flow from small surface waves, then recombines their velocities. Its demonstrated CUDA setup uses a 512-square grid at one-metre spacing. The paper reports 128 diffusion substeps per update and assigns 87% of simulation time to decomposition. Sections 4.1 and 5 document those choices; section 5.2 also identifies missing surface tension/physical viscosity and limitations around fine seabeds and boundaries. These results do not establish browser cost. The method's water-grid scale and missing small-scale effects make a complete port a poor first experiment for this site's rain.

The model reviewed extracted method/appendix text. The coordinator independently checked the central scale, decomposition-cost and omitted-force claims against the primary publication. Direct download failed in the model worktree; no PDF-byte hash or independent figure-pixel inspection is claimed. Its extraction evidence stays outside Git. No author code or redistribution license was adopted.

At that proposal baseline the rain field had 1,024 desktop or 256 compact lines, a four-metre-per-second fall and a seven-second active-time cycle. The controller supplied terrain/roof heights without exposed-water ownership. Those are historical source observations. The current [ocean contract](../../../../assets/js/home-scene/PHYSICS.md#rain-contact-and-small-wave-detail-november-2026) describes the replacement: diameter-dependent trajectories, first downward contacts, rendered-grid sampling and distinct Pacific/pool ownership. It preserves analytic large waves, bathymetry, optics and reflections.

The [local implementation checkpoint](../../reviews/rain-contacts-2026-11-02/README.md) retains the seeded trajectory audit, actual complete-scene contact comparison, matched paused normal controls and source-bound production checks. All 652 observed water events match a frozen unbounded reference tracker; conservative height bounds reduce queries by 73.8%. The ocean response combines bounded gravity-capillary normal packets with a separately authored estimate of dense small-wave forcing and pixel-footprint filtering. It is an original browser approximation, not a port of the reviewed shallow-water solver.

Complete production Chromium and modern Docker/WebKit contact checks pass locally. Native RTX 3080 Ti measurements show similar GPU cost in the matched rainy view, but its whole-frame median remains about 50 ms. This does not establish a speedup or acceptable overall performance. Physical-iPhone cost remains open. Pool rain response, crown spray, wave coupling and volumetric water are not implemented.
