## The living floor in graphite

The source is the visitor’s actual 26.5-second frame from the House film. A conical steel hood interrupts one corner of the pale sofa. The island slats and the chair backs lead to the two pendants. Keeping those relationships fixed made it possible to judge what a pencil could leave out. In the first drawing, the fire appears as an erasure in the densest graphite. There is just enough line on the cushions to suggest their weight. The hatch across the hood still feels straight for a spun piece of steel. It is the first thing to inspect as the camera moves. Generated with the built-in image generator on November 20, 2026. The WebP is a downsample of the saved original. The prompt used for the original follows.

> Square full-bleed front cover artwork for a real twelve-inch chamber-folk record, flat on paper viewed square on. The only lettering is the title “A Pocketful of Sky”, set beautifully in small dark indigo book serif in the quiet upper left. Design a single bold, graphic image that is readable from across a room: a molten burnt-sienna La Jolla sandstone cliff folds around a nearly square opening of intense cornflower-blue Pacific sky, with a ruler-straight indigo sea horizon crossing the opening. The sandstone face is partly the hollow bell of an enormous carved walnut music horn, suggested by sweeping carved grain, not a drawing of audio equipment. Warm transparent persimmon and copper washes against cool ultramarine and cream paper. One tiny golden orange reflected inside the dark cut; a good-humoured, assured energy under a calm exterior. Handmade relief-print and restrained watercolour on fine-grained warm ivory sleeve stock, crisp cut edges against one bleeding shadow. Make the negative space and one sculptural silhouette do the work. A sophisticated original independent record jacket, tactile, spare, luminous. No record mockup, vinyl disc, scene outside the paper, people, logos, numbers, extra text, or imitation of any existing album. Square texture asset.

### Panorama prompt

> Create a single original texture atlas for an interactive music and art control on a coastal architect's home. Landscape overall with width about 1.5x height. Exactly FIVE equally tall edge-to-edge horizontal panoramas stacked perfectly one atop another, no margins, no text, no captions, no lettering, no divider lines, no UI, no button, no frames. Each row is a broad 7.5:1 image, same composition and camera: a cutaway cross section of an inhabited midcentury house carved into amber sandstone on the La Jolla Pacific cliffs. At far left distant cobalt sea and windswept Torrey pine; left centre sunlit reading room with books and small record sleeve; middle a blue ocean opening and low half-round ochre sofa; right centre luminous circular stone disc, walnut horn speakers and turntable built into dark rock; at far right honey light out onto a terrace above surf. Large unmistakable silhouettes, bands of warm/cool, calm buoyancy and life, space, visually arresting at the scale of a tiny instrument dial, more finely observed joinery in a magnifier. FIVE genuinely different drawing idioms of THE SAME panorama, top to bottom: 1. beautifully photographed tiny physical architectural model, directional low amber light and believable texture. 2. hand-painted gouache blocks in burnt sienna and lapis, dry brush. 3. expressive translucent watercolour on warm paper with ink-violet shadows and glowing apricot hearth. 4. restrained two-ink coastal woodcut, indigo and ochre, hand-carved uneven lines. 5. charming bold cel animation with midnight blue ink, coral, sand, joyous and slightly whimsical. Line up geological and window silhouettes between rows. Draw the architecture and music objects only, with exact five equal strips. The long skinny output strips are production artwork used under actual glass lens.
