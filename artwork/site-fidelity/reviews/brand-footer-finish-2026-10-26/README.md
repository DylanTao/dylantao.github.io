## Brand question

The earlier attempt left a generic round disk with an accessory leaf and a decorative scratch. The new alternatives change the silhouette, proportion and sea relationship, rather than polishing that scratch. They apply Susan Kare's small-grid, meaningful-metaphor and iterative-selection lessons, as credited in `WEBSITE_DESIGN_HEURISTICS.md`.

- **A / Cut-paper cove:** a slightly shouldered orange with a broad open wave cut and a connected crown. One ink color carries most of the mark.
- **B / Pacific fruit:** a generous orange sun above a deep teal sea, held in one compact silhouette. The sea is a positive shape rather than a fine white stripe.
- **C / Low sun:** a wider, lower citrus sun meeting a level horizon, with an offset wave below it. Its proportions emphasize sunset rather than a round app badge.

Small-size and navbar rendering, peer critique, selection, failures and verification will be recorded here before either box is closed.

The coordinator's first peer critique rejected A–C: A retained the sliced-disk structure, B mostly recolored the lower slice and became muddy on navy, and C read as a weather/flag icon. This is a peer design judgment, not a user study or Sirui's approval. They are retained as unsuccessful attempts.

The second family adds a shouldered mandarin contour and top dimple, a full leaf attached by a short stem, and a broad sea relationship:

- **D / Mandarin cove:** one orange mass remains connected around a tapered, open-ended wave carve.

- **F / West-facing leaf:** a left-facing crown and broader tide change the balance and sunset proportion.
