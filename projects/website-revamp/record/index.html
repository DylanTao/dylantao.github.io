Inter in five type roles

Shipped · Sep 14, 2026

Every page uses Inter in five roles (page title, section title, subsection, body, metadata and controls), each a computed size token.

How it works, files and sources

_sass/_themes.scss defines --type-display, --type-heading, --type-subheading, --type-reading and --type-compact as clamped sizes and keeps older names as aliases. Inter replaced the Newsreader display face on September 14.

A 33.5rem reading column

Shipped · Oct 12, 2026

How it works, files and sources

_sass/_reading.scss sets --reading-width: 33.5rem for prose and --reading-wide-width: 68ch for figures and code, and the contents rail and trace pages follow it. A sun you can drag

Shipped · Oct 13, 2026

The theme panel is a day arc. Dragging the sun picks any minute, the theme snaps to that hour’s mode, and the page’s sky glow and the 3D scenes follow.

How it works, files and sources

assets/js/sun-arc.mjs maps minutes onto a drawn day arc, with a flatter night arc for the moon (sun-arc-math.mjs); since October 19 it maps the real day onto that reference day piecewise, so dusk meets the horizon and solar noon the crown. The four token sets still carry contrast; only the panel and a sky glow at the top of the page shift color continuously, the glow moving left to right with the sun. The footer coast takes its look from the sun’s altitude, and the 3D home previews the hour when the drag ends, through a siteTimeChange event. Now hands everything back to the clock. A theme chosen anywhere else moves the sun but never sets a preview hour. This is direct manipulation in Shneiderman’s sense: a continuous representation, physical action and immediate, reversible feedback.

One clock on La Jolla’s real sun

Shipped · Oct 19, 2026

The site’s time-of-day theme, the sun arc and the footer coast all keep La Jolla’s real sun for the visitor’s date, so the site turns to evening as dusk falls on the coast, at a different minute each day, instead of at 8 pm all year.

How it works, files and sources

la-jolla-sun.mjs computes the sun at 32.83° N, 117.27° W with NOAA’s general solar position equations (fractional year, equation of time, declination, hour angle), sunrise and sunset at −0.833°, civil twilight and solar noon (solarPosition, sunEvents), with the visitor’s clock read as La Jolla’s and a US daylight-saving rule standing in where Intl lacks long offset names (pacificOffset, usPacificOffset). Noon is the four hours around solar noon, and evening begins with the sun 3° down, the altitude at which light ink reads as well as dark on the coast behind the footer’s credit, measured on the afterglow (themeDay, themeModeAt). theme.js keeps an exact copy for the head (laJollaThemeDay) so the theme is right before any module loads, and the sun arc maps the real day onto its drawn reference day piecewise (toReference, fromReference).

Springs named by their damping

Shipped · Oct 12, 2026

Buttons and menus answer a press with a damped spring instead of a fixed ease, from two named springs.

How it works, files and sources

_sass/_springs.scss defines a confident spring (ζ 0.8, settling in 0.48 s with 1.5 % overshoot) for buttons and menus and a playful one (ζ 0.55, 0.9 s, 12.6 %) for the desk toys. bin/spring_easing.cjs samples a damped oscillator’s step response for any damping ratio and period into a CSS linear() curve, describing springs by bounce and duration as Apple’s spring guidance does. The theme menu enters with @starting-style, and reading disclosures open through ::details-content. Browsers without linear() keep a similar cubic Bézier.

A clear sea along the footer

Shipped · Oct 18, 2026

How it works, files and sources

Crests travel shoreward every nine seconds, close up as they shoal and break into lacy whitewater over the shelf (surfPhase, breaking), and the boards and the swimmer trail Kelvin wakes (wakeArm). A small render of the scene from the camera’s mirror image below the water covers only the band where water shows and is redrawn on demand, like the shadow map, so steady frames cost no extra draws (aimMirror, createReflectionSchedule); ripples sway it, and only light above a theme threshold streaks far.   7. Page weight and blocking time

    Needs a decision Visual QA and checks

    Measured on the live site on October 18, 2026 with Lighthouse 12.8.2’s desktop preset (median of three runs), the homepage scores 61 with a 1.85 s Largest Contentful Paint (the eager portrait), 903 ms of total blocking time and 1.8 MB in 128 requests; paper-research-field.js spends 826 ms of script mounting its WebGL field as soon as its noise texture arrives, on screen or not, and GSAP ScrollTrigger about 0.6 s with the layout it triggers. DesignWeaver scores 80 with a 1.69 s LCP but a 0.17 layout shift, the whole article moving once.     • Minify the ES modules, which jekyll-terser skips (the site’s 31 non-3D modules would go from 110 to 72 KB gzipped).
    • A content-hashed Service Worker cache, since GitHub Pages’ cache lifetime cannot be configured.

Keep intact

  • Decision order. Research meaning, credit, accessibility and a working route come before first-glance clarity, which comes before proof proximity, interaction state, and finally atmosphere and delight.
  • Keep the signature copy exact, including “Making AI tools that sharpen design judgment.” and “Design, Evaluate, Situate.”
  • Brand orange is identity only. Links, controls and focus use the active time-of-day accent, and the four palettes carry contrast.
  • Every effect is a complete still page under reduced motion, Pause motion, without a fine pointer, and under automation unless the URL carries ?cinematic=live.
  • No scroll or wheel hijacking, cursor trails or ambient loops; parallax only between two layers that belong together.
  • Human and AI routes stay separate surfaces. AI routes are compact, source-linked and motion-free, and the switch between them is an ordinary link.
  • Research figures stay whole and uncropped, with declared dimensions.
  • When a review calls an element confusing, remove it rather than tune it.
  • New font sizes use a type role; the raw font-size ceiling in the style contract stays at 333.
  • Iterate on one route, state and viewport; checkpoint explicit routes at four viewports; run the full matrix only for a release.
  • Render-cost and weight changes must not change the pixels a visitor sees.
