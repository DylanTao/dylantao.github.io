Light paths through the onsen

Experimental · Oct 14, 2026

A plain-JavaScript renderer races path tracing against Metropolis light transport on the sunlight the light well bends through the onsen’s water.

Its own page: Light Paths in the Onsen

How it works, files and sources

createRenderer in core.mjs builds one path sampler that reads every random decision from a fixed coordinate of a sample vector. PathTracer feeds it fresh numbers and MetropolisRenderer feeds it Kelemen and colleagues’ small and large mutations, so the two estimators share every line of path construction. Both use next-event estimation to the sun and sky with power-heuristic multiple importance sampling, after Veach, and water hits use safeguarded Newton iteration on seven frozen ripples. The page runs each estimator in a module worker for the same wall time, and make_light_lab_reference.mjs splits path space at the first hit for a 24-minute reference.

A light well with traced shaft light

Shipped · Oct 14, 2026

A shaft drilled from the clifftop brings sun into the onsen, and its beam, its sun patch, its skylight and the lit steam share one visibility function.

How it works, files and sources

bin/coastal_lightwell.py drills a shaft of 0.42 m radius through 1.67 m of rock, leaning 28° toward the noon sun. createLightWell in light-well.mjs follows four light paths. The sun’s shadow map carries the patch on the floor or the water. Dust and humid air in the beam are single scattering with a forward Henyey–Greenstein phase, marched only inside the shaft’s elliptic cylinder, after Wronski. shaftExitRadiance path-traces the drilled cylinder on a fixed Halton sequence for the radiance leaving the aperture, and the aperture and the sun patch then light the bath as two small area lights in closed form (wellIrradianceGLSL).

Traced sky and lamp bounce

Shipped · Oct 2, 2026

A CPU ray tracer bakes sky visibility and one diffuse bounce into probe cages once, then relights them from the actual sky and lamp powers without tracing again.

How it works, files and sources

createStaticLightField builds a triangle BVH over the house’s static opaque meshes (createTriangleBVH), traces eight cosine-weighted sky rays per primary hit, and stores irradiance as first-order spherical harmonics (accumulateL1, evaluateL1) in 56 probes with octahedral distance moments, after Ramamoorthi and Hanrahan and the probe visibility of Majercik and colleagues. Unit sky and lamp bases let a change of hour recombine the cache with zero rays. The field replaces three.js’s ambient terms once; direct light, reflections and refraction stay native. The bake runs in yielded slices and frees its accelerator afterwards.

Horizon-based contact light

Shipped · Oct 1, 2026

A visibility buffer computed before the beauty pass darkens only indirect diffuse light near contacts, with the closed-form horizon integral of ground-truth ambient occlusion.

How it works, files and sources

contact-occlusion.mjs projects the geometric normal into four fixed view-aligned slices and integrates the cosine-weighted visibility above each slice’s two horizons in closed form (horizonIntegral), Jimenez and colleagues’ inner integral, with at most 32 depth probes per pixel. A 3 × 3 reconstruction keeps neighbours on the same tangent plane (bilateralWeight), and the paper’s cubic fit adds back the light a pale neighbourhood bounces (bounceVisibility). bindContactLighting scales only each material’s indirect diffuse term, so sunlight, emission and reflections are untouched, and a still composes the same way every time.

A Pacific of Gerstner waves

Shipped · Oct 1, 2026

Ten Gerstner waves displace the sea and its normals together, with a wind sea that grows with the breeze, gusty ripples and whitecaps, a lit shoreline and a live mirror of the cliff.

How it works, files and sources

sampleOcean in ocean-spectrum.mjs sums ten choppy Gerstner components from 24.7 m to 6.1 cm with gravity–capillary dispersion and analytic tangents, after Finch, and keeps the steepness sum under 0.3 so the surface never folds. Each amplitude fades with the pixel’s footprint, and the slope energy it loses widens the GGX lobe, a scalar version of Bruneton, Neyret and Holzschuch’s transition from geometry to BRDF. Since October 16 the seven shorter components are a wind sea that turns and grows with the coast’s breeze under a Pierson–Moskowitz cut (windSeaScale), pixel ripples follow the local gusty wind by the Cox–Munk slope law (rippleScale), and whitecaps cover Monahan and Ó Muircheartaigh’s fraction (whitecapCoverage). createPacific adds swash, wet sand and lit foam, and createSeaReflection renders a mirror with exact water Fresnel.

A height-field onsen

Shipped · Oct 2, 2026

The onsen is a 40 × 40 shallow-water height field whose waves carry water between cells, reflect off the rim and the bather, and start from the bather’s hand.

How it works, files and sources

createHeightfieldWater follows section 2.1 of Chentanez and Müller: staggered heights and face velocities, conservative upwind depth fluxes, pressure from the free-surface gradient and reflecting solid faces, at fixed 1/120 s ticks with CFL subdivision; total volume holds to about 10⁻¹⁴. measurePoolGeometry measures the real basin, about 0.127 m deep. createPoolStroke drives a slow hand skim, and the palm’s submerged velocity relaxes nearby face velocities inside each step, so a resting hand adds no energy. createOnsenWater packs elevation, slopes and wet weight into a texture for the pool’s material and runs only when the camera can see the pool.

Thermal steam

Shipped · Oct 2, 2026

Bath mist is two transported fields, droplet concentration and temperature, rendered with single scattering that composes in order around the glass and the water.

How it works, files and sources

createSteamDensity advects an 18³ concentration and temperature field at a fixed 30 Hz with midpoint semi-Lagrangian backtracing, after Stam, and lets warmer cells rise faster, after Fedkiw, Stam and Jensen’s separate smoke density and temperature; diffusion and cooling use exact exponential factors, and since October 16 the drift follows the house’s share of the coast’s breeze (setWind). createSteamVolume marches up to 40 samples with Beer–Lambert extinction and a Henyey–Greenstein phase (integrateSteamSamples, steamPhase), after Wronski, and splits each ray once at the nearest glass or water (steamRayIntervals) so the gas in front and behind composes in order.

One wind for the coast

Shipped · Oct 16, 2026

One breeze, measured at Scripps Pier and carried by gust fronts that travel downwind, moves the sea, the clifftop planting, the room plants, the curtains, the steam and the particles.

How it works, files and sources

createCoastWind in wind-field.mjs runs a periodic cubic Hermite through the October hourly means at Scripps Pier (NDBC LJPC1, 2023–2025) and writes every gust as a function of retarded time t − x·d/c, so a front reaches each place in turn at the mean wind’s speed, Taylor’s frozen turbulence. An exact integer hash makes the shader chunk (coastWindGLSL) and the CPU agree on every gust. Indoors, a six-mode curl-noise field adds the house’s own eddies (sampleWind), exactly divergence-free, after Bridson, Hourihan and Nordenstam. patchFloraSway bends the planting by a lean that grows like U^1.4, as streamlining leaves’ drag does, and a sway at each species’ own frequency phased by retarded time, so gusts roll across the bluff; the room plants bend around their real stems.

Sun position and sky

Shipped · Oct 1, 2026

Sheer curtains in the breeze

Shipped · Oct 16, 2026

Four sheer panels inside the glass front, a position-based cloth in the coast’s breeze, with a weave whose openness, glow and shadow change with the angle.

How it works, files and sources

createCurtainCloth steps 29 × 25 particle panels with small-step extended position-based dynamics: colour-batched structural, shear and bend constraints, long-range tethers, and a kinematic heading of carrier rings whose sine folds gather or spread. Air acts per triangle from the wind relative to the cloth, a flat-plate normal force plus skin friction applied semi-implicitly, and createFacadeDraft feeds jets through an open door from the coast’s onshore breeze. weaveOpenness treats the voile as a plain weave of round yarns, so its gaps close toward grazing angles; back light passes as a thin Lambertian sheet plus a forward Henyey–Greenstein lobe, and a dithered shadow pass leaves a partial pool of light. createCurtainRig steps panels on screen at 30 Hz, and curtainSchedule draws them against the low sun.

Bedding draped by position-based dynamics

Shipped · Oct 14, 2026

Each avatar’s duvet is dropped over the sleeping body offline by a position-based cloth solver and its pillow pressed in by the head, so no cover cuts through a chest; in the scene the cover breathes.

How it works, files and sources

draped_duvet in bin/coastal_bedding.py builds a height field of everything under the cover (support_field: sleeper, pillow, mattress, frame and bedside table) and drape drops a cloth grid onto it with coloured Gauss–Seidel distance constraints and a soft bend, after Müller and colleagues and Fratarcangeli, Tibaldo and Pellacini; the cover is then trimmed across the chest, quilted and given 3 cm of fill. sleepers poses every avatar’s sleep clip at its deepest and shallowest breath, and pillow presses each pillow wherever a sleeping head rests. bin/refresh_coastal_bedding.py rebuilds the six bed variants in the retained house. In the scene, createBreathingCover in bedding-breath.mjs lifts the sleeper’s cover a few millimetres along its normals around the chest at about fourteen breaths a minute, inhaling over 40 % of each breath (breathAt).

Shipped · Oct 15, 2026

Open problems

  1. Which paper first

    Needs a decision How the lab is kept

    Every watchlist entry would make a technique more faithful, but they differ by an order of magnitude in effort and in how much a visitor would notice. The shortlist proposes four with a visible payoff; the choice is a taste call.

    • Sun and sky: a scattering sky, for real sunsets and a terrace hour at dusk.

  2. Simulation without compute shaders

    Needs a decision Water, air and sky

    The scene renders through WebGL2 and three.js r164, which have no compute shaders. An FFT ocean, a pressure solve for the steam, cloth on the GPU and probe updates all want them, so each must become chains of fragment passes or wait for WebGPU, a move that would touch every material and pass the shader warmup knows.

    Candidate moves

    • Prototype one FFT pass chain as fragment passes on a separate page and time it against the authored spectrum.
    • Keep the small solvers (the onsen, the steam, the cloth) on the CPU and move only the ocean’s spectrum to the GPU.
    • Price a WebGPU renderer for the home as its own experiment before any paper depends on it.

    • Tessendorf, Simulating Ocean Water

  3. Light that only a tracer can find

    Needs research Light

    Candidate moves

    • Precompute a caustic pattern in the lab and project it in the onsen, moved by the solver’s slopes.
    • Shade the light well’s aperture with glossy area lights, the cheapest specular path the house is missing.
