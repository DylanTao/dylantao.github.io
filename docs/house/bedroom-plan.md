# The bedroom, as a room: a plan on paper

The first step of docs/todo.md, item 5 (creative direction, brief 4). Written by Claude as creative director on November 17, 2026. Build from it in the same architecture pass as the speakeasy (item 4), and change it when the room says otherwise.

**His words.** "the bedroom can be bigger and much better well designed instead of just having a bed"; November 3: "when i am sleeping, my bedroom should maybe have curtain".

## The idea

A bedroom is for waking up to the sea and winding down at night. Today it is a bed in a room. It needs places for the hours around sleep: reading in bed, sitting by the window with coffee, dressing, putting things down.

## Layout

- **The bed** faces the glass, so the sea is the first thing seen on waking; a low platform in pale oak, linen bedding, a wool throw at its foot.
- **The headboard wall** behind it, in warm plaster: a shallow niche the width of the bed for books and a glass of water, two small reading lights set into it, warm and aimed at the pillows.
- **By the glass:** one deep reading chair and a small round table, angled to the view, on a wool rug.
- **To dress:** a wall of closed cabinets in the same oak with a bench in front, on the side away from the glass, and a tall mirror leaning against the wall.
- **The curtain** he asked for: full-height linen on a ceiling track along the glass, drawn when he sleeps (the routine already knows when that is), open by day.
- **Planting:** one tall plant in a terracotta pot by the chair; one trailing plant on the niche.
- **Bigger:** carve about a metre and a half into the rock behind the headboard wall, for the cabinets; the light bake's budget (B1) decides how much.

## Light

By day, the sea's light through the glass, softened by the curtain's edge. At night, low and warm: the two reading lights, a floor lamp by the chair, a faint wash along the headboard wall's niche. No light from above at night.

## Sirui in it

Sleeping (the curtain drawn, the room dark but for a sliver of moonlight at the curtain's edge), reading in bed, and coffee in the chair at the glass in the morning: three activities the routine can choose between.

## How it is judged

From inside at 7 am (the curtain open, the sea bright, coffee in the chair) and at 11 pm (the curtain drawn, reading lights on), and from the sea at night (a warm, shielded glow, not a lit box), beside the references. It is done when the bed is one of several places to be.
