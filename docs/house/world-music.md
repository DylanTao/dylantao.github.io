# Music for the coastal house

On November 20 Sirui asked for a different original piece for each way the house is drawn. He heard the fifth violin solo as crowded, sharp and sad. The quality he wanted from Paganini was the lift of a difficult gesture. From folk guitar and the Beatles he wanted wit and a pulse you could move to. From the opening of _Runaway_ he wanted the time to recognize a sound before something else answers it.

## The five scores

| World       | Piece                            | Players and shape                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ----------- | -------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Watercolour | _A Pocketful of Sky_, 42 s       | A quiet G on the acoustic guitar establishes a pulse. The violin enters after two bars with three long descending notes. The thumb starts answering on the offbeat halfway through. In bar 15 the guitarist leaves the downbeat to one recorded D–E–D bow and returns underneath the long answer. The opening G comes back at the end. |
| Realistic   | _The Water on the Sill_, 27 s    | Cello and guitar in three. The low guitar notes hold the floor while the cello rises to D in its middle register, then finds its way back to the opening chord.                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Painted     | _Copper in the Last Light_, 24 s | Warm viola bow against the short glints of a mandolin. The flattened seventh gives the sustained phrases an unsettled colour that resolves on A.                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Woodblock   | _Five Cuts in Pearwood_, 24 s    | Mandolin and guitar group five beats into three and two. Longer empty spaces separate the pairs of attacks like the intervals between cuts in a block.                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Cartoon     | _The Spare Key_, 20 s            | Plucked violin and banjo trade short questions in two. A whole bar goes missing from the accompaniment before the answer comes back.                                                                                                                                                                                                   |

The melody, timing, harmony and arrangements are original. Each sounding note comes from a recording of the named instrument at that pitch and speed. The player brings the same room reflections into each duet. The signature violin part has 23 entrances in 18 bars, with one ornament in the open space left by the guitar. The level rises across several bars through the middle. A future arrangement can change that emotional timing by editing the phrase onsets, accompaniment and dynamic arc together. ## Interaction

The visitor owns the first sound. A full piece belongs to time spent in a world. A page or room interaction takes only a small fragment of the same instrumental language. The fifteen responses reuse tones from the corresponding composition. A choice gets two light notes, a page-section arrival opens an interval, and a room threshold gets a three-note answer. A long scroll or a sequence of rapid choices needs space between responses. The walker's shoes, oars, water and training equipment have a separate physical sound.

## Reproducing a take

Keep the Philharmonia Strings ZIP outside this checkout. The Python renderer uses NumPy, SciPy and imageio-ffmpeg. Pass the archive to `python bin/render_coastal_world_music.py --strings <Strings.zip> --output assets/audio/coastal-score --review <private-directory>`. It writes the five finished pieces, the cue sprite and its timed index. The review directory receives the lossless mixes and the onset, source file and SHA-256 for each played event. The source musicians' individual samples stay in their archive.

For a revision, listen through the guitar entrance, the change in rhythmic feel and the landing as one performance. Compare at the same playback level. Check the attack and the fundamental early in each source note. A dying guitar string can ring most strongly at an overtone. On this take the dominant harmonics of six flagged guitar files were measured again in their first half-second and matched the score. The judgment still open is whether the music carries the house's energy for Sirui.
