Read as zones, from the house outward, all facing the ocean: the indoor gym (lifting, the rower and bike at the glass, the bags); a wide threshold that opens fully, with planting and a waterfall, so inside and outside read as one Japandi garden; an open lawn for yoga, Pilates and stretching, with room for boxing and the rower or bike outside on fine days; and, carved into the rock further right, a small niche with a hot spa and a cold room. Elegant rather than gym-bro: natural timber, stone, water, low planting, equipment of good design kept few and put away when not in use. The speakeasy's side of the house gets its own outdoor room (an outdoor dining terrace with the BBQ, grill and fridge by the pool, todo item 4), so the two outdoor rooms have different jobs: eating and gathering there, moving and resting here.

The sea is the reason to train in this room, so the cardio faces it, in the light; the bags take the dim side, where hitting something feels right. The rack and platform stay where they are: his lifting already works there (the bar loading and warm-up of November 13 and 14).

## Layout

- **At the glass:** a rower and a bike side by side, each on its own dark rubber mat, both facing the sea, 0.9 m apart so the camera can pass between them. The rower runs perpendicular to the glass (about 2.4 m long), the bike beside its front end. Nothing taller than the bike's bars stands between them and the view.
- **The bag corner,** on the side away from the glass: a heavy bag (about 1.2 m, 35 kg) on a chain from a ceiling mount, with 1.2 m clear around it; a double-end bag on cords from ceiling to floor; a speed bag platform on the wall at head height.
- **Along the wall between them:** a low shelf with gloves, hand wraps, a skipping rope and two towels; a water bottle on the floor by the rower.
- **Light:** daylight from the glass by day; at night a low warm wash along the floor by the machines (the sea stays visible) and one warm pendant over the bag corner.

## Sirui in it

- **Rowing:** a full stroke cycle (catch, drive, finish, recovery) at about 24 strokes a minute, seat, handle and chain moving together; the handle never passes through his body.
- **Riding:** about 85 revolutions a minute, standing for a few bursts.
- **Bag work:** jab, cross, hook combinations on the heavy bag; rhythm on the speed bag.
- Audit each with every avatar for a handle, pedal or glove through the body, as the bars were (H5).

## Small physics

- The heavy bag swings as a damped pendulum (a 1 m chain swings with a period of about 2 s) when struck, and settles within a few seconds.
- The double-end bag springs back between its cords.
- The rower's fan and the bike's flywheel spin down after use over several seconds.
- All of it holds still under reduced motion.

## Building it

Real sizes in Blender, made in code like the rest of the house; generic machines with no maker's design or mark copied. Check the light bake's triangle budget first (B1). Give each machine an activity in the manifest and its own sound later (item 8).

## How it is judged

From inside at 7 am (the sea bright through the glass, Sirui rowing) and at 9 pm (the floor wash, the pendant over the bags), and from the sea, beside the references. It is done when the room reads as used, and the empty floor is gone.
