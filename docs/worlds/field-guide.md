# The field guide world: rule sheet

A one-page contract to agree before any shader (docs/todo.md, item 12; takeover notes J6 and J8). Written by Claude as creative director on November 17, 2026. Change it when Sirui's eye says so.

## What it is

Sirui, November 13, naming the drawn styles he likes, with a poster of _Common Side Effects_ attached. On every drawn world: "they should not just cheaply do a trivial shitty transform". The mood for the home: calm and beautiful, never eerie.

So: the house drawn like a plate in a naturalist's field guide, with the deadpan calm of that show's manner. One thin line, flat dusty colour, an off-white ground, and one or two things that glow. On the site it is named for how it is made, Field guide; what is taken from the show is technique, never its characters, logos or frames.

## How such a drawing works, and what that asks of the renderer

- **One thin line,** slightly irregular, as if drawn with a fine pen, the same weight everywhere; it describes, it does not dramatise.
- **Flat colour, dusty and natural:** sage, ochre, clay, faded blue, never pure. Each surface is one colour.
- **One shadow shape.** A single darker version of the surface's colour, with a clean edge, where the light does not reach. No gradients.
- **Marks only where they explain a texture:** a few lines for wood grain, dots for sand, short strokes for grass, and nothing on plain walls.
- **An off-white ground** where nothing is drawn, like the paper of a printed guide.
- **One or two saturated, glowing accents** per view: the lantern, the fire, the pool's light. Everything else stays quiet so they sing.

## The rules

1. **A line of one weight,** wobbling a little along its length, fixed to the object so it does not shimmer.
2. **Each surface gets its own flat colour and one shadow colour,** taken from a small palette of dusty naturals.
3. **Texture marks are sparse** and sit on the surface; never a pattern over the whole picture.
4. **At most two glowing accents in frame,** chosen for the view's subject.
5. **Deadpan timing:** ordinary physics, long holds, nothing bouncy. Calm is the joke and the beauty.

## His figure, a companion, sound

Sirui drawn as a specimen plate would draw a person: still, exact, a little dry. A companion that a field guide would label: a small coastal creature (a lizard on the warm rock, a gull), drawn with the same line. Sound: quiet, close, the room's real sounds with a lot of silence.

## What must not change

The house, its rooms and layout, walking and every control, accessibility and reduced motion, and the realistic render as the default.

## How it is judged

In three views: the kitchen nook by day, the living room at night with the fire, and the garden from above. It is ready when a frame could be a plate in a good illustrated guide, and still feels like home.
