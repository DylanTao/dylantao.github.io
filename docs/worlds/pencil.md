# The pencil world: rule sheet

A one-page contract to agree before any shader (docs/todo.md, item 12; takeover notes J4 and J8). Written by Claude as creative director on November 17, 2026. Change it when Sirui's eye says so.

## What it is

Sirui, November 13: pencil, "black and white with different gray ... truly floaty feeling". On every drawn world: "they should not just cheaply do a trivial shitty transform". The mood for the home: calm and beautiful, never eerie.

So: the house as an architect's or illustrator's pencil drawing on warm white paper, light on its feet. Graphite only, many greys, a lot of untouched paper, and a sense that the whole drawing floats.

## How pencil drawing works, and what that asks of the renderer

- **Line first.** A confident contour, heavier where a form turns away and on the side away from the light, lighter and broken where light falls on an edge (the cartoon's structure pass already finds where lines go).
- **Tone by hatching.** Greys are built from parallel strokes that follow the form, crossing for darker values, with paper left between them. Tone comes from stroke density, not from grey fills.
- **Lost and found edges.** An edge in full light can disappear into the paper; the eye completes it. Leaving things out is what makes it float.
- **Vignette.** The drawing thins toward the sheet's edges and stops before them, as a sketch does on a page.
- **The paper's tooth.** Graphite catches the paper's grain, more in darks; the grain is fixed to the sheet.

## The rules

1. **Graphite on paper only.** No colour, except perhaps the site's orange once, as a single coloured-pencil accent on the subject of a view.
2. **Five values at most:** paper, light hatching, mid, dark, and the darkest accents, used sparingly.
3. **Hatching follows form** and is fixed to surfaces (like the painted look's strokes), so it never swims when the camera moves.
4. **Light comes from one side.** Lit planes are paper; turned planes hatched; cast shadows cross-hatched with a crisp edge.
5. **The view's edges dissolve** into paper and sketch lines; the subject is drawn fully, the rest suggested.
6. **Floaty:** objects sit on faint shadows rather than heavy ground; slow, gentle motion; nothing hard or mechanical.

## His figure, a companion, sound

Sirui drawn with a few certain lines and light hatching, a little looser than the house. A companion as a doodle in the margin that wanders onto the page. Sound: pencil on paper, soft and dry.

## What must not change

The house, its rooms and layout, walking and every control, accessibility and reduced motion, and the realistic render as the default.

## How it is judged

In three views: the living room by day, the study at night with its lamp, and the coast from the sea. It is ready when a frame could pass for a page from an architect's sketchbook.
