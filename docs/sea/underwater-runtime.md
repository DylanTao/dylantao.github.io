# Diving off the coastal house

In the full-screen home, Walk down to the beach and swim out until **Dive** appears. The button and **C** begin a dive. The ordinary Walk movement and look controls carry over. Move forward while looking down to swim deeper. **Surface** or **C** rises at the current horizontal position. **Space** on the canvas or the controller's jump button also returns to the surface during a dive. A touch visitor gets Dive and Surface above the existing Look stick. Reduced motion keeps kelp, fish, flecks, and light still during deliberate swimming.

Dive eligibility comes from the displaced Pacific surface and rendered coast capture. The same Walk capsule still sweeps against the native scene and excludes the tide pools and infinity pool from the dive calculation. The ocean bed beyond the last captured triangle continues the existing model's offshore grade. Placement there is an authored habitat within the bounded cove. The offshore grades and habitat are interpretive models.
