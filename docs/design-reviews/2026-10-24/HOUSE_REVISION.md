## Intended experience

The dwelling belongs inside the cliff. Its pool should read as a light coastal
court supported by actual rock, with an easy dry route from the living room. The
main arrival descends directly into that living room; a discreet separate route
serves the onsen. One curved internal stair replaces the duplicate living/onsen
connections. The clifftop gains a comfortable place to sit and enjoy the view.

The study is one tall library, with a rolling ladder for its high shelves, the
complete laptop/PC workspace, a warm hearth and a compact spiral connection from
the bedroom wing. The upper floor becomes private living and rest. Rolling is an
actual interaction; a climbing animation is not part of this checkpoint.

**Bedroom adjacency remains partial:** the delivered route has about 4.2 m of
bedroom approach and 7.4 m of upper-level travel before the spiral, including a
5.05 m bridge. It does not meet Sirui's request for immediate proximity without a
long connection. Moving the stair within the current room gains less than a
metre; an adjacent stair needs a new chamber, floor openings and a new circulation
review. Preserve this as a specific architectural follow-up rather than calling
the current link short.

Curtains frame the panorama rather than close the public rooms at night. Fitted
planting, warm wayfinding/cove light and composed bathroom stonework keep the
coastal home recognizable. The computer setup, records, books, hearth and quiet
private quarter express Sirui's daily life. This revision does not change public
portfolio copy, Inter, the orange/coastal identity, papers or analytics.

## Delivered changes

- **Rock-supported coastal court:** only the dwelling moves eight metres
  landward; the retained mainland and coast stay fixed. The pool occupies an
  excavated rock basin, with the former deep perimeter girder and three exposed
  piers removed. The dry living-to-patio route reaches the pool-entry coping.
- **Clear public and private arrival:** a curved 48-tread descent reaches the
  lower living room through a proximity-operated oak pocket door. The discreet
  private spiral reaches the onsen without its former glass-door obstruction.
  One bowed 16-tread internal stair replaces the duplicate connection. Railings,
  supported landings and actual openings carry the route through the architecture.
- **A lived-in library:** the complete laptop/PC desk, records and associated
  setup move into one tall book room, with a recessed stone hearth and a rolling
  ladder. The former upper office becomes a private chaise/chill space. The
  ladder rolls; it is explicitly nonclimbable.
- **An outdoor place to linger:** a lower, lighter cedar arrival shade and a
  slatted clifftop conversation pavilion give the entrance places to sit and
  enjoy the ocean. The retained patio entertainment and pool shelf remain
  accessible components of the scene.
- **View-first interiors:** public curtains gather at the glazing returns at
  night and on a room visit. Fitted Strelitzia, vanity Asplenium and trailing
  library Epipremnum replace arbitrary doorway planting. Bathrooms gain a
  limestone border, larger dry-zone stone cuts, finer flush shower cuts and
  concealed vanity light. Existing coves retain a stronger night guide level.

## Weather scope

[Weather provenance and validation](../../../artwork/coastal-home/weather-2026-10-24/README.md)
records NOAA history, the date-limited NWS forecast, model selection, chronological
holdout and expiry. The small runtime artifact requires no visitor-side weather
requests or ML weights. A seasonal Markov baseline improves one-day held-out
probabilities over monthly climatology; this does not establish SOTA prediction or
validate the synthetic long-range scene sequence. The forecast falls back to
explicit seasonal simulation outside its actual published intervals.

Rain uses at most 512 exterior line segments, or 256 in the compact profile, no
extra rendering pass and no shadow caster. Native roofs/covered volumes and
terrain stop the field. Its positions follow active time and hold when motion
stops; the existing live clock may still refresh weather and illumination while
Pause holds animation. [Desktop clear/rain evidence](../../../artwork/coastal-home/reviews/retreat-integration-2026-10-24/reports/desktop-weather.json.gz)
and the isolated mobile receipts above use the retained natural forecast
intervals: October 24 clear and October 30 at 12:30 PDT rainy. They inspect the
real position buffer, roof shelter and draw resources, with no RAF, timer or
performance-clock acceleration. Rain adds no pass or shadow caster; other
existing effects retain their own passes. This is useful local scene weather,
not a SOTA forecasting claim.

## Successor priorities and remaining limits

1. **Finish publication custody:** local final geometry, cache, drawings, production build and browser proof pass below. Append exact commit/CI/deployed verification after publication; do not borrow the earlier release's 41-job result.
2. **Resolve actual bedroom adjacency if Sirui still wants it:** preserve the
   measured 4.2 m approach plus 7.4 m upper travel as partial fulfillment. A new
   nearby stair chamber or revised footprint needs fresh floor, guard, human and
   companion proof. Renaming the existing bridge does not solve the distance.
3. **Make a deliberate laundry decision:** the playful cloth still crosses the
   pool close-up. An optional service-bay relocation must move posts, line,
   constraint/render frames and camera/wind bounds together, then be visually
   reviewed. It is not silently removed or declared finished by this revision.
4. **Refine excavation only with a bounded architectural goal:** the heavy
   cantilever impression is reduced, but faceted exposed geology remains visible.
   Organic rock transitions would require actual new geometry and updated support
   and route proof, rather than disguising it with light or camera placement.
5. **Measure mobile performance on real hardware:** the compact rendering profile
   and touch viewport have functional evidence; smoothness, battery use and real
   touch navigation remain unmeasured on a phone. Start from one representative
   device and trace a specific bottleneck before adding rendering complexity.

Keep the durable engineering lesson: a housing-frame change must reach CPU
routes, GPU planes, materials, fixture cages, mirrors, perches and cloth exactly
once. Native support, source closed solids, delivered compression and a rendered
view answer different questions. Preserve dated failed witnesses and require the
evidence for the changed representation, without treating a large test count as
a substitute for the intended experience.
