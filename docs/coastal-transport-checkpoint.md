# Coastal light and fluid transport — October 2, 2026

This checkpoint improves the existing realistic room through bounded custom transport, without changing its public controls, characters, GLBs, homepage narrative or 2D player. Integration lives in `controller.mjs`, `realism.mjs`, `environment.mjs` and `coastal-wind.mjs`; three small independent numerical implementations carry their own tests and equations.

## Research and implemented scope

| Transport | Primary basis                                                                                                              | Actual implementation                                                                                                                  | Limits                                                                                                                                                |
| --------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Skin      | [Christensen and Burley, 2015 normalized diffusion memo](https://research.pixar.com/docs/2015.TechnicalReport.CB.pdf)      | Original positive radial quadrature, curved-patch RGB preintegration, metric normal-derivative curvature; direct diffuse lookup only   | Local convex-surface approximation; pointwise shadows; no spatial shadow/albedo diffusion or thickness transmission                                   |
| Pool      | [Chentanez and Müller, SCA 2010, section 2.1](https://matthias-research.github.io/pages/publications/hfFluid.pdf)          | Staggered shallow-water heights/velocities, conservative upwind flux, pressure gradients, reflecting bath/torso masks, CFL subdivision | Constant shallow bottom, dissipative first-order velocity advection, prescribed one-way torso; no breaking fluid, buoyancy or two-way hand coupling   |
| Wind      | [Bridson, Hourihan and Nordenstam, SIGGRAPH 2007](https://www.cs.ubc.ca/~rbridson/docs/bridson-siggraph2007-curlnoise.pdf) | Analytic curl of a bounded six-mode vector potential; rooted critically damped plant response; RK2 tracer/droplet motion               | Fourier basis replaces Perlin noise; procedural divergence-free wind, not a pressure-projected Navier–Stokes simulation; no actual building collision |

These are original browser adaptations of established research, not faithful reproductions of whole papers or a claim of current SOTA. Detailed equations and source relationships live in [skin diffusion](../assets/js/home-scene/SKIN-DIFFUSION.md), [onsen water](../assets/js/home-scene/ONSEN-WATER.md) and [coherent wind](coherent-wind-equations.md).

Skin uses RGB distance parameters 3.2/1.2/0.65 mm and a 257×49 RGBA16F lookup (100,744 bytes), shared per finish. Hair, glasses and ordinary cloth keep their surface response. The existing shirt primitive enables diffusion only when the soak wardrobe changes it to bare skin. Released avatars return their borrowed material handles; no fullscreen skin pass is added.

Pool geometry is measured from the actual loaded water mesh: world center approximately [3.17,3.18], radius .86 m, top3.007513 m. Five interior rays resolve the flat native stone bottom at2.880026 m, giving .127487 m depth; missing or nonflat receivers expose a labeled illustrative fallback. The numerical grid is40², fixed1/120-second active ticks with CFL subdivision. The round top carries simulated displacement/slopes in beauty. A prescribed slow left-hand skim now supplies measured submerged palm velocities to the momentum equation inside each CFL step. Resting hands inject no force; the former periodic height pulses are removed. The .17 m torso footprint retains excluded volume; this remains an illustrative mask rather than anatomical swept-volume fluid coupling.

Pool optics use Three r164's existing dielectric transmission, IOR1.333, roughness.065, transmission.86, measured vertical basin thickness.127487 m and attenuation distance1.5 m. This requires a background color pass when transmissive water is in view. Its screen-space refraction/Beer–Lambert attenuation is engine functionality, not newly implemented paper code. It can miss offscreen background; one vertical depth is still an approximation to the optical path. The numerical adapter itself adds no fullscreen pass. The interface neither casts an opaque floor shadow nor participates as an opaque normal-depth AO occluder. Refractive caustics and transmitted-shadow transport are omitted.

Plant attachments are built once in world meters from the actual honey-ash supports, then converted through each mesh's TRS.110 olive components bend;22 ambiguous hanging components and unsupported agave remain rigid. Roots and a1.8 cm neighborhood stay pinned; an inverse-transpose Jacobian corrects normals. Beauty and custom depth/distance shaders share the same bend. The bounded indirect contact buffer uses static leaves, a documented mismatch below12 mm. No geometry deformation or attachment scan occurs per frame.

Existing particle counts remain18 vapor,42 dust and140 surf droplets. Deterministic prewarm produces a mature still arrangement. Their world positions evolve with the same active-time wind field; droplets also integrate gravity and drag. Lifetime/boundary respawn, buoyancy and opacity remain authored approximations. Hidden, offscreen, paused and reduced-motion frames do not advance these solvers. After a slow frame, water/particles accept at most.25 seconds, report discarded time and retain less than a tick; they create no catch-up queue.

## Recent research checked

[Neural SSS, CGF2024](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/cgf.15158) handles directional/heterogeneous transport beyond diffusion, but depends on trained fixed object/optical representations and reports18–84-second renders on an RTX4090. [Leapfrog Flow Maps, SIGGRAPH2025](https://yuchen-sun-cg.github.io/projects/lfm/static/pdfs/SIG_2025_Leapfrog_Flow_Maps.pdf) evolves genuine volumetric vortices using flow history and pressure projection; its real-time examples use CUDA–Vulkan interop/RTX4090, including a14.1 ms256×128×128 vortex step. Neither paper demonstrates a drop-in WebGL/mobile implementation for this scene. Selecting the bounded existing techniques is an engineering judgment based on the current visual scale and budget, not a claim that newer research is inferior.
